Everything for the month — the way salary works.

Paid on the 27th? Your month runs 27th to 26th. Every screen, widget and Wallet pass follows your cycle.

📊

What's left, at a glance

Income, planned and current remaining, unpaid bills and expenses for the cycle — plus a 12-cycle history.

🧾

Bills you can tick off

Due days, categories, every month or selected months. Reminders and calendar export so nothing slips.

💳

Credit cards, without the numbers

Track what's owed and what's left of each limit. Card bills are created for you. No card numbers stored.

📆

Installments made easy

Tamara, Tabby, bank or car loans — plans create the payments and catch up in one tap.

🎯

Savings & goals

Set a target, watch progress, and choose which goal pays when you spend from savings.

🏢

End-of-service estimates

UAE and GCC rules or your own formula, with advances deducted — built for life in the Gulf.
