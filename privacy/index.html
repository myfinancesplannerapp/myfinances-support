Privacy Policy

My Finances Planner
Last updated: 21 November 2026

Overview

My Finances Planner is a personal finance planning app for iPhone, iPad, Mac, Apple Watch and Android. This policy explains what data is handled and how. The sections below apply to all versions; the Android app section covers what is specific to Android.

Data we store

Financial information you enter — income, payments, expenses, installments, savings and goals, credit card nicknames and limits (never card numbers), salary and end-of-service details, and profiles — is stored on your devices. When iCloud sync is enabled, that data is stored in your personal iCloud account via Apple CloudKit so it can sync across your devices signed in with the same Apple ID.

Apple Wallet pass

When you choose Add to Wallet, the app sends a summary of the current cycle to our pass server so the pass can be created and signed: your remaining balance, savings total, number of unpaid payments, billing cycle, profile name, and each card’s nickname with its remaining and used amounts. The server uses this only to build the pass and sends it straight back to your device. It does not store this information. Nothing is sent unless you use Add to Wallet.

Exchange rates

When you refresh exchange rates, the app downloads current rates from open.er-api.com. No personal or financial information is sent.

What we do not do

  • We do not sell your data.
  • We do not use your financial data for advertising.
  • We do not keep your financial data on our own servers. Your records live on your devices and, if you turn on sync, in your own iCloud account (Apple) or your own Google Drive, encrypted (Android).

Authentication

Optional Face ID, Touch ID, or PIN is processed on your device. PIN storage uses the device Keychain where implemented.

Apple services

The app may use Apple services such as iCloud/CloudKit, notifications, calendar access (if you enable export), and Wallet-related features on supported platforms. Those services are governed by Apple’s privacy policy and your Apple ID settings.

Backups and export

You can export a JSON backup from Settings. It contains all your profiles and their data. Files you save, including to iCloud Drive, are under your control.

Android app

The Android version of My Finances Planner works the same way: the information you enter is stored only on your device. There is no account, no advertising and no analytics, and we do not run a server that receives your data. The Apple Wallet pass and Apple services described above are not part of the Android app.

Google Drive sync (optional). If you turn on Google Drive sync, the app stores one encrypted file in a hidden app folder in your own Google Drive, so your Android devices can stay in step. The file is encrypted on your device with a passphrase that only you know, before it is uploaded. Google and the developer cannot read it. The app can only see its own folder, not the rest of your Drive. You can turn sync off, or delete the file from Drive, in Settings → Google Drive Sync. If you forget your passphrase it cannot be recovered.

Android backup. Android may include the app's data in your device's encrypted backup to your Google account, under your Android backup settings. Your sync passphrase and sync key are not included.

Permissions.

  • Notifications: only for the payment reminders you turn on.
  • Calendar: only when you choose Export to Calendar, to add your due dates to your calendar.
  • Internet: to download daily exchange rates from open.er-api.com (nothing about you is sent) and, if you use it, for Google Drive sync.

App lock. Fingerprint, face unlock and your screen lock are handled by Android; the app never sees them. If you set an app PIN, only a salted hash of it is stored on your device.

Widgets. Home-screen widgets show figures from your data on your home screen, including while the app lock is on. Remove the widgets if you don't want amounts visible there.

Moving between iPhone and Android. Use Export Backup on one device and Import Backup on the other. The backup file is not encrypted; keep it somewhere private and delete copies you no longer need.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13.
